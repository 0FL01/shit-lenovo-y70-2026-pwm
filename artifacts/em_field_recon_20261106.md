## Consequence for the next experiment

The strongest new hypothesis is a **brightness-range selector**, not a discovered
frequency register. A narrow stock-only optical comparison immediately below/above
DBV3601 could test for a regime boundary without changing unknown registers; the
previous2839/5197 comparison was too widely spaced to locate that boundary.
It would not by itself prove which pair owns it or certify2000Hz.

A future isolated field experiment must distinguish threshold selection from pulse
timing, keep a complete known-good target set for restoration, and be proposed as
an unverified experiment—not packaged as a verified PWM profile. No hybrid command
set, raw-register writer or Android persistence mechanism has been implemented.

The remaining blocker is causal field semantics: the emitted composite programs
are known, but their pulse-count/timebase encoding is not. No new profile can yet
be labelled >=2000Hz. This is not evidence of hardware impossibility; it is the
boundary between verified bytes and a still-unverified experimental modification.
