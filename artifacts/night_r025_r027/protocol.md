Plan fixed before this replay, after R10 labels and original results were public. Base d4c56fb. No devices, no capture, no private pixels published. Units are pixels of the saved preview, not sensor pixels or time.

- R025: ten original R10 raw per-frame classifications at fixed R10 sampling coordinates and row thresholds. Compare 8 square orientations allowing all 64 cyclic phases (repeating pattern); known polarity primary. Exact top score must be unique to claim orientation + phase per frame. Report nearest alternative and check synthetic constant and checkerboard controls for exact ties.
- R026: locate internal two-dimensional source corners only (the local 2×2 pattern changes along both axes). Start from original A1-only integer affine geometry. Search translations ±5 px in x/y, quarter-pixel spacing, with fixed quadrant template and 11×11 image samples, positive NCC. Reject edge maxima or NCC <0.75. Use A10 only, (column+row) even, to solve a homography. Use B0 and B1 separately, odd parity only, with disjoint corner coordinates and changed code, to **test**, never update the fit. Require ≥6 accepted non-degenerate training points, ≥6 test correspondences per frame, and all test corners found within 2 preview px of model to support a local 2-px transfer claim; report missing corners and errors, not just a pass. B frames not independent series. Model also compared with original affine prior.
- R027: top/bottom horizontal scale from the fitted H, along the central four-cell span x=2…6 at y=1 and 7, check whether there is empirical support from B odd-parity landmarks. For each B frame, estimate horizontal pitch independently in rows 1–2 and 6–7 as the median of all same-row detected landmark pairs separated by at least 2 cells. Require both bands in both frames for an empirical comparison. Quantify local transfer error by translating a copy of the top H horizontal segment to the bottom H left endpoint; report max tested bottom B residual and missing coverage. Neither a fitted homography nor the largest observed residual guarantees a bound outside the sampled locations; no scene-wide, hard physical bound without independent coverage.
